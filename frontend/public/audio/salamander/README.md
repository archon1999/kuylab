# Salamander Grand Piano V3

Recorded piano samples by **Alexander Holm**, licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). See LICENSE.txt.

The Yamaha C5 piano was sampled in minor thirds over 88 keys. This application includes three velocity layers (v5, v10, v15), 30 pitch anchors each, in MP3 format as distributed by [Tone Piano](https://github.com/tambien/Piano/tree/0cd2c034f820c53e83ab22f5c13bd490b9e4de85/audio). Original bank: [Salamander Grand Piano V3](https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3). License and authorship are also documented by the [FreePats distributor](https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html#SalamanderGrandPiano).

Downloaded 2026-10-11 from the pinned commit above. MP3 files are unmodified. Playback changes pitch by at most one semitone from the nearest anchor, selects the velocity layer, applies MIDI velocity and duration, and adds adjustable synthetic room reverb. The original piano recordings are not an oscillator simulation. No endorsement by the sample author is implied.

`manifest.json` contains paths, byte sizes and SHA-256 hashes for every bundled file. There are 90 samples, totaling 16,815,297 bytes. The bank is served locally and cached for offline use. No third-party request is needed to play.
