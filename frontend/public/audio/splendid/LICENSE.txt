Splendid Grand Piano — sampled Steinway
Original author: AKAI. Samples: Public Domain.
Source: https://sfzinstruments.github.io/pianos/splendid_grand_piano/
Original archive: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SplendidGrandPiano
Unmodified OGG distribution: https://github.com/smpldsnds/sfzinstruments-splendid-grand-piano
226 recordings, all four velocity layers. Local filenames replace spaces and sharps with URL-safe characters; audio bytes are unchanged. Pitch mapping uses upstream MIDI numbers, not the filename octave convention.

smplr 1.1.0 by danigb: MIT (as declared in the npm package).
https://github.com/danigb/smplr
Piano mapping and velocity curve are used from the public package.
Dattorro AudioWorklet DSP is from that package, originally the following project.
KuyLab modifications add reset/dispose messages only; DSP/default parameters unchanged.
